<h1 class="section-heading">{% include section-icon.html name="about" %}<span>About Me</span></h1>

I am a Ph.D. student at the [School of Statistics, Renmin University of China](http://stat.ruc.edu.cn/), advised by [Professor Hanfang Yang](http://isbd.ruc.edu.cn/English/Faculty/75f7b9495f664db2b47dce10253a84ff.htm). My research focuses on **training data resources synthesis and evaluation for large language models**, as well as **large language model reasoning**.

I received my bachelor's degree from [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/) and pursued both my master's and Ph.D. studies at Renmin University of China. I am currently a research intern at [Microsoft Research Asia](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia/), working with [Zhenghao Lin](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=sLshrmQAAAAJ&hl=en), [Xiao Liu](https://xiaoliunlc.github.io/), and [Yeyun Gong](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/yegong/).
